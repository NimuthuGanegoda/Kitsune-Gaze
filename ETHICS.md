# Ethical Code of Conduct - Kitsune-Gaze

Kitsune-Gaze is built on the principles of transparency, user empowerment, and absolute privacy. As a public tool, we adhere to the following ethical guidelines:

## 1. Non-Malicious Use
This tool is designed strictly for personal security awareness. It must never be used for doxxing, stalking, or any form of unauthorized surveillance.

## 2. Data Sovereignty
Users have full control over their data. We do not store, sell, or trade any personal identifiers (emails, usernames, etc.) provided during the search process.

## 3. Transparency
Our methods for breach detection are open and verifiable. We use publicly available data sources and reputable APIs (like HaveIBeenPwned) to provide accurate information.

## 4. Free for All
Security should not be a luxury. Kitsune-Gaze will always remain free of charge for the public, ensuring that everyone can protect their digital presence regardless of their financial status.

## 5. Responsible Disclosure
We encourage the community to report vulnerabilities in our own systems so we can maintain the highest standards of security.
