# Privacy Policy - Kitsune-Gaze

Your privacy is the foundation of our work. This policy outlines how Kitsune-Gaze handles your data.

## 1. No Data Retention
Kitsune-Gaze is designed to be **stateless**. We do not save your search queries, IP addresses, or result history to any database. Once your session ends, your data vanishes.

## 2. Compliance
We strive to comply with global data protection standards, including:
- **GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation):** We respect the "Right to be Forgotten" and the principle of data minimization.
- **CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act):** We do not sell personal information.

## 3. Third-Party API Usage
To provide accurate breach data, we may query external services. When doing so:
- We only send the minimum required data.
- We prefer services that support k-anonymity (e.g., hashing the first 5 characters of a password/email).

## 4. Encryption
All communication between the frontend and backend is encrypted via HTTPS (in production).

## 5. Contact
If you have concerns about your data, remember that we don't have it. But if you'd like to talk about the project's architecture, you can reach out to the maintainers.
